# Brief — L-MRG-WASTE-PANEL-SAYS-ABSENT

export PLAN_ACTOR=agent:L-MRG-WASTE-PANEL-SAYS-ABSENT

## Objective
a feature that does not exist reads as absent rather than as broken

**The panel calls four routes no backend checkout here publishes**, and **swallows the 404 into "unknown"** —
so a venue is shown a **read that failed** where the truth is a **feature that is absent.**

**That is the worse of the two possible wrongs, and the reason this is a blocker rather than a polish item.**
A missing feature invites a question. **A failed read invites a retry, then a support call, then distrust of
the numbers standing beside it on the same screen** — and those numbers are the venue's margin.

**Decide which of the two exits you are meeting and say so.** Either the routes exist somewhere and the panel
should call them, or they do not and the panel must say absent. **Do not split the difference with a friendlier
error string** — that keeps the failed-read reading and only softens it.

**Prove it by making a 404 arrive** and showing the panel says absent, with the test reding when the swallow
returns. A panel that renders correctly against a fixture that never 404s has not been tested at all.

**Estate hazards:** never run `npm ci`/`npm install`; keep scratch lane-local; commit by explicit pathspec; a
fresh worktree needs the file-protocol override to initialise the submodule, and without it **six** suites red
for a reason that is not yours.

## Exit criteria

## Verdict — sound-with-conditions

**The three test-only pin lanes are sound, mutually compatible, and their seven-read accounting is exact.**
The four-way merge is textually clean and **leaves none of the seven strengthened pins unfalsifiable** —
cross-masking is structurally impossible, because grace pins the worker's decrement, the other two pin the
authority's, and **no pin ever sends the supersede token**, so the re-quote lane's new release cannot fire
inside any of them.

**The condition is the fourth lane. It introduces a brand-new guard-decrement site whose clamp-to-zero
direction is unfalsifiable by every committed test — the exact defect shape this cluster exists to kill,
reintroduced on the same day.**

## 1. The seven-read accounting — confirmed, and there is an eighth

Nine zero-shaped assertions exist at the base. **Seven are the claimed degenerate reads, covered once each,
no overlap, none missed.** The other two are genuinely out of shape.

One correction to the lanes' own records: the floor-pins evidence cleared a read as *not vacuous* which is
**wrong** — the value it asserts is exactly what a clamp also produces. **Grace-pins caught that in its own
survey and degenerate-two fixed it.** The system corrected itself; the floor-pins survey should not be
cited as authority.

**The eighth read exists in the merged state, introduced by the re-quote lane.** Its superseded-release is a
**third production decrement site**, and the reviewer walked a clamp mutant through all nine of its tests
and the browser journey: **every one passes.** In every test where the release fires, the superseded
reservation is the only other hold, so **clamp-then-increment lands on exactly the asserted value.**

The exact remedy is named: hold one **uninvolved** quote across the supersede, so correct and clamped
diverge — plus a refusal variant where the re-quote fits from zero but not over the uninvolved hold.

## 2. Merge verdict

**Textually clean at all four tips**, verified stepwise. The two lanes editing adjacent regions auto-merge,
and their trial artifact genuinely shows all four authority pins red under a clamp.

**Semantically composed by construction** — different code paths, and a release that requires a token no pin
sends. The mutation signature is right everywhere: **every red artifact reads zero, never the residual.**

**Sweep candidacy: every lane used the right kind** — and the reviewer corrected the framing I had been
repeating. An unbound *expired* quote **is** a sweep candidate; the hazard is that only a *bound* one files
the exception. Grace's holds are minted after the clock moves; degenerate-two's is unbound and released
before it; floor's pins run no sweep at all; and the new release only takes reserved, unexpired, owned rows,
so it **removes candidates and never creates one.**

**The audit gap stays visible** — every pin asserts reason codes only. But **the new release is a fourth
site writing no audit row**, and the blocked actor lane's worklist enumerates exactly **three**, proposing a
kind vocabulary with **no kind for an ordering member**. **That worklist is now stale**: merging the
re-quote lane without amending it means the eventual fix lands three-of-four and the new site stays silently
unaudited.

**Order:** the two shared-file pin lanes first, then grace, then **re-quote last** — and only with the
uninvolved-hold clamp pin, the actor worklist amended to four sites, a full fast tier on the merged tree,
and a decision about a regenerated journey artifact that one lane's evidence says is absent from its commit
and **is not.**

**Two of my prompt's claims corrected.** *Nobody has looked at all four together* is false in the narrow
sense — the re-quote lane committed a four-way trial, and those runs are genuine for what they contain.
**It is true in the sense that matters: no full tier and no mutation has ever run on the four-way state.**

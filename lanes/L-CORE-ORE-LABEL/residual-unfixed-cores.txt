"suffix-fix" = priceLabelTool suppresses the ",–" notation when a fraction is printed.

WHAT IS STILL WRONG IN THE THREE THAT WERE LEFT, AND WHAT IT COSTS
The suffix half is harmless in Web and AdminApp: both call setCurrencyFormat with suffix "" at
startup (Web plugins/global-mixin.js:55, AdminApp app/main.ts:246), so neither could render
"206,80,–". That is the whole reason the ruling could be consumer-only.

The øre half is NOT harmless and is still live on both admin surfaces. fractionAmountTool widens a
single-digit minor amount to "00", so an amount of 1-9 øre renders as though it had none: 4 øre
prints "kr 0,00" on a screen an operator reads. It is a smaller defect than the consumer one and it
was never this lane's exit criterion, but it is a money-display defect that is now knowingly left in
two shipping clients plus the standalone Core, and it should be somebody's lane rather than a thing
three Cores quietly disagree about.

AdminApp additionally carries hand-written duplicates of wholeAmount/fractionAmount in
app/main.ts (found by L-CORE-ORE-LABEL-1, not touched by either pass), so fixing its pin alone would
not fix its own copy.

WHY NOT JUST BUMP THEM
Three Core branches, and two of the feature/swiss histories are divergent local trees: ConsumerApp's
pin is not an object in ConsumerWeb's copy, and both carry commits with identical subjects under
different hashes. The fix therefore had to be authored three times (4f31003, c0d70a4, a1ce983) --
byte-identical in the changed region, three unrelated commits. That is the cost the ruling accepted
and the reason converge-core was recorded as its own scoped work rather than folded into a rendering
fix.

REOPEN WHEN (from the ruling): a defect appears in a client that was left pinned, or the four -- now
six -- Core histories are converged for another reason.
